# Split primes and composite norm values

Original section and equation numbers are retained.

The first extract treats a cutoff below $M_{\rm lo}^3$; the second explains the larger cutoffs and the endpoint tests used for products of two or three primes. Here $M(D)=(D+1)/4$, whereas $M_{\rm tri}$ denotes the number of rows. The references to implementation functions identify the corresponding operations; they do not replace the displayed mathematical inclusion and valuation arguments. The original formula allows four factors, although the application in the main paper uses only two and three. If a primitive norm has $r$ distinct split prime factors, the stated multiplicity is $2^{r-1}$, irrespective of their exponents. The function $S_r$ and its monotonicity are proved in note 02. The earlier progression estimate with its additional hypothesis $S\leq X$ is omitted; that hypothesis is not required by the product argument retained here.

For $D \in [D_{\text{lo}}, D_{\text{hi}}] \subset [875, \, 3,968,539]$ with $D_{\text{lo}}, D_{\text{hi}} \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$, set $M(D) := \frac{D+1}{4} \in [M_{\text{lo}}, M_{\text{hi}}]$ ($X(D) = M(D) - 1 \geq 218$) and extend the prime cutoff to $P_{\max} \in \{1,699,999, \, 8,999,999\} \subset (X(D), M_{\text{lo}}^3)$ (since $M_{\text{lo}}^3 \geq 219^3 = 10,503,459 > 8,999,999$). Every prime $p \leq X(D)$ is inert in $\mathcal{O}_K$ (in particular all primes $p < K \leq 31$ are unconditionally inert since $X(D) \geq 218$). At any split prime $p \in (X(D), P_{\max}]$ ($p\mathcal{O}_K = \mathfrak{p}\bar{\mathfrak{p}}$, $\mathcal{O}_K/p\mathcal{O}_K \cong \mathbb{F}_p \times \mathbb{F}_p$), every $z_i \in \mathcal{O}_K$ satisfies $\bar{z}_i^p - \bar{z}_i \in p\mathcal{O}_K$, so the monic degree-$s$ polynomial $\tilde{A}_s^{(p)}(z') := (z')^{\tilde{d}_0}\prod_{\ell \geq 1} G_\ell(z')^{\tilde{d}_\ell} \in \mathbb{Z}[z']$ formed from the base-$p$ digits $\tilde{d}_\ell$ of $s$ via $G_1(z') := (z')^p - z'$ and $G_{\ell+1}(z') := G_\ell(z')^p - p^{p^\ell-1}G_\ell(z')$ realizes the split-prime row valuation $v_p(\tilde{A}_s^{(p)}(\bar{z}_i)) \geq \sum_{m \geq 1}\lfloor s/p^m\rfloor = v_p(s!)$ with zero derivative loss under $\mathcal{D}_z^{(j)}$, while the unitriangular basis $\tilde{P}_{r,s}^{(p)}(z, z') := z^r \tilde{A}_s^{(p)}(z')$ has zero column valuation loss ($b_{p,\text{split}}(j) = 0$ instead of $-\lfloor j/p\rfloor$). Thus switching a prime $p > X(D)$ from inert to split replaces $w_r(p)\log p = \frac{S_r(R, K, p)\log p}{K W_{\text{main}}}$ by the odd-power $s$-gain $s_{\text{gain}}(p)\log p := \frac{2K\log p}{W_{\text{main}}}\sum_{\ell \geq 1}\Sigma_{\text{tri}}(S, p^{2\ell-1})$ (retaining the even powers $p^{2\ell}$ already in $L_{\text{padic}}$) and retains the first-power Legendre saving $-\lfloor j/p\rfloor\log p$ in $-\log(j!)$ across all columns $(i, j) \in C$. By Lemma 4.2(2), $p \mapsto w_r(p)\log p$ is strictly decreasing on $[3, R]$, so any norm interval $p \in [p_{\text{lo}}, p_{\text{hi}}] \subset [3, \infty)$ satisfies the exact endpoint enclosure $w_r(p_{\text{hi}})\log p_{\text{hi}} \leq w_r(p)\log p \leq w_r(p_{\text{lo}})\log p_{\text{lo}}$. Since $\mathcal{O}_K = \mathbb{Z}[\omega]$ is a UFD with $\mathcal{O}_K^\times = \{\pm 1\}$ and every split prime $p = \pi\bar{\pi}$ has four generators $\{\pm\pi, \pm\bar{\pi}\}$ whose coordinates $(u = 2x+y, y)$ lie in the four distinct sign quadrants $(\pm, \pm)$, and since $M(D) = \frac{D+1}{4}$ is odd for $D \equiv 3 \pmod{8}$ so $N_D(x, y) := x^2 + xy + M(D)y^2 \equiv x^2 + xy + y^2 \equiv 1 \pmod{2}$ is automatically odd for every pair $(x, y) \not\equiv (0, 0) \pmod{2}$ (and in particular whenever $\gcd(x, y) = 1$), every split prime $p \in (X(D), P_{\max}]$ occurs as $p = N_D(x, y) \in [p_{\text{lo}}(x, y), p_{\text{hi}}(x, y)]$ for a **unique** primitive pair $(x, y) \in \mathcal{N}_D(P_{\max}) := \{(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z}^2 : y \geq 1, u = 2x + y \geq 0, \gcd(x, y) = 1, N_D(x, y) \leq P_{\max}\}$ with $(x, y) \neq (-1, 2)$ (while $(-1, 2)$ on the imaginary axis $2x + y = 0$ gives the unique ramified prime $N_D(-1, 2) = D$). Furthermore, because no prime $q < M(D)$ can divide $N_D(x, y)$ for $(x, y) \in \mathcal{N}_D(P_{\max})$ and $P_{\max} < M_{\text{lo}}^3$: (i) every composite norm in $\mathcal{N}_D(P_{\max})$ is a product $m(D) = p_1(D)p_2(D) \in [m_{\text{lo}}, m_{\text{hi}}] := [p_{1,\text{lo}}p_{2,\text{lo}}, p_{1,\text{hi}}p_{2,\text{hi}}]$ of **exactly two** guaranteed primes $p_1, p_2 \leq \lfloor P_{\max}/M_{\text{lo}}\rfloor < M_{\text{lo}}^2$ with generators $2z_k = u_k + y_k\sqrt{-D}$ ($k=1,2$), whose products $z_1 z_2$ and $z_1\bar{z}_2$ have exact first-quadrant coordinates $(u_+(D) = |u_1 u_2 - D y_1 y_2|/2, \, y_+ = (u_1 y_2 + u_2 y_1)/2)$ and $(u_-(D) = (u_1 u_2 + D y_1 y_2)/2, \, y_- = |u_2 y_1 - u_1 y_2|/2)$ (with first-quadrant multiplicity $1$ for $\pi_1^2$, $2$ for $\pi_1\pi_2, \pi_1\bar{\pi}_2$ with $\pi_1 \neq \pi_2$ split, and $1$ for $\sqrt{-D}\bar{\pi}_1$); and (ii) every pair $(x, y) \in \mathcal{N}_D(P_{\max})$ with $p_{\text{hi}}(x, y) \leq q_{\text{cap}} := \min(Q_{\text{guar}}, M_{\text{lo}}^3 - 1, J - 1)$ whose coordinate $(u = 2x+y, y)$ does not lie in any composite interval $[u_{\pm,\text{lo}}, u_{\pm,\text{hi}}]$ on line $y_\pm$ is **unconditionally prime** for all $D \in [D_{\text{lo}}, D_{\text{hi}}]$. For this set $\mathcal{G}_{\text{slope}}$ of guaranteed primes, $-\lfloor j/p(D)\rfloor\log p(D) \leq -\lfloor j/p_{\text{hi}}\rfloor\log p_{\text{lo}} = -j\frac{\log p_{\text{lo}}}{p_{\text{hi}}} + \frac{j \bmod p_{\text{hi}}}{p_{\text{hi}}}\log p_{\text{lo}}$, where $-j\frac{\log p_{\text{lo}}}{p_{\text{hi}}}$ is absorbed into $\text{slope}_j^{\text{hi}}$ and the joint remainder sum $\sum_{(i,j)\in C} H(j)$ of the 1D array $H(j) := j\,\sigma_2^{\text{lo}} - \text{PP\_FL}[j] - \sum_{(p_{\text{lo}},p_{\text{hi}})\in\mathcal{G}_{\text{slope}}} \lfloor j/p_{\text{hi}}\rfloor\log p_{\text{lo}}$ ($0 \leq j < J$) is bounded across all $|C| = M_{\text{tri}}$ by sorting $H(0), \ldots, H(J-1)$ in non-increasing order and summing $n$ copies of the top $\lfloor M_{\text{tri}}/n\rfloor$ values plus $(M_{\text{tri}} \bmod n)$ copies of the next value.

### Composite norm products

Finally, in Stage 4 (`certify_hybrid_k3_interval` in `supplement/verify_euler_window_and_863_ladders.py`), setting $\text{max\_factor} := \min(\lfloor P_{\max}/M_{\text{lo}}\rfloor, M_{\text{lo}}^3 - 1)$ on intervals (or $\lfloor P_{\max}/M_{\text{lo}}\rfloor < M_{\text{lo}}^4$ on discrete $D_0$, where every reducible primitive $\alpha = x + y\omega \in \mathcal{O}_K$ of norm $\leq \text{max\_factor}$ factors as $\pi_1\beta$ with $N(\pi_1) \leq \sqrt{\text{max\_factor}} < M_{\text{lo}}^2$ and primitive $\beta \in \text{tier1\_cands}$ of norm $\leq \lfloor\text{max\_factor}/M_{\text{lo}}\rfloor$, so `comp2_grid` sieves all reducible elements up to $\text{max\_factor}$) and $q_{\text{cap}} := \min\big(Q_{\text{guar}}, \, M_{\text{lo}}^2(M_{\text{lo}} - dM)^2 - 1, \, J - 1, \, \text{max\_factor}\cdot M_{\text{lo}}\big) < M_{\text{lo}}^4$ ($dM := M_{\text{hi}} - M_{\text{lo}} \in \{0, 1\}$) guarantees that every composite in the slope window $\mathcal{S} = \{(x, y) \in \mathcal{N}\setminus\{(-1, 2)\} : p_{\text{lo}}(x, y) > X_{\text{lo}}, \, p_{\text{hi}}(x, y) \leq q_{\text{cap}}\}$ has $\Omega(m) \in \{2, 3\}$: specifically, for any 3-prime composite $m = p_1 p_2 p_3 \leq q_{\text{cap}}$, all three prime factors satisfy $p_{i,\text{lo}} \leq \lfloor q_{\text{cap}}/M_{\text{lo}}^2\rfloor \leq (M_{\text{lo}} - dM)^2 - 1$ and $p_{i,\text{hi}} \leq p_{i,\text{lo}} + dM\lfloor 4p_{i,\text{lo}}/D_{\text{lo}}\rfloor < M_{\text{lo}}^2$ (so all three belong to `guaranteed_primes`), while for any 2-prime composite $m = p_1 p_2 \leq q_{\text{cap}}$ ($p_1 \leq p_2$), the smaller factor satisfies $p_{1,\text{lo}} < M_{\text{lo}}(M_{\text{lo}} - dM) \implies p_{1,\text{hi}} < M_{\text{lo}}^2$ (in `guaranteed_primes`) and the larger factor satisfies $p_{2,\text{lo}} \leq \lfloor q_{\text{cap}}/M_{\text{lo}}\rfloor \leq \text{max\_factor}$, belonging either to `guaranteed_primes` (`is_guar = True`) or—when $dM = 1$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ is composite at some integer step in the interval—to `extra_sf_cands` (`is_guar = False`).   Crucially, **any primitive lattice point $(X, Y)$ ($Y \geq 1$) with lower-endpoint norm $p_{\text{lo}}(X, Y) < M_{\text{lo}}^2$ is unconditionally irreducible across every integer discriminant $D_* = 4M_* - 1 \in [D_{\text{lo}}, D_{\text{hi}}]$** (*Universal Irreducibility Lemma*): indeed, since $\frac{4M_{\text{lo}} - 1}{4}Y^2 \leq (X + Y/2)^2 + \frac{4M_{\text{lo}} - 1}{4}Y^2 = p_{\text{lo}}(X, Y) < M_{\text{lo}}^2$ forces $Y^2 < \frac{4M_{\text{lo}}^2}{4M_{\text{lo}} - 1} < M_{\text{lo}} + 1$ and hence $Y^2 \leq M_{\text{lo}}$ by integrality, one has $N_{D_*}(X, Y) = p_{\text{lo}}(X, Y) + (M_* - M_{\text{lo}})Y^2 < M_{\text{lo}}^2 + (M_* - M_{\text{lo}})M_{\text{lo}} \leq M_{\text{lo}}^2 + 2(M_* - M_{\text{lo}})M_{\text{lo}} + (M_* - M_{\text{lo}})^2 = M_*^2$, whereas every non-unit $\gamma \in \mathcal{O}_{K_{D_*}} \setminus \mathbb{Z}$ has norm $N_{D_*}(\gamma) \geq M_*$ so any reducible primitive element would have norm $\geq M_*^2$. For candidates in $[M_{\text{lo}}^2, \text{max\_factor}]$ on width-4 intervals ($dM = 1$), two norm curves $N_D(X, Y) = p_0 + (M - M_{\text{lo}})Y^2$ and $m(D) = p_1(D)p_2(D)$ can cross or invert across $M \in \{M_{\text{lo}}, \ldots, M_{\text{hi}}\}$, so checking only sub-interval inclusion $[p_{\text{lo}}(X, Y), p_{\text{hi}}(X, Y)] \subseteq [m_{\text{lo}}, m_{\text{hi}}]$ would not suffice to guarantee reducibility across the integer discriminants in $[D_{\text{lo}}, D_{\text{hi}}]$. Instead, with `discrete_comp2 = True`, `certify_hybrid_k3_interval` constructs a bitmap `comp2_grid` indexed by $(Y, u_X)$ across every discrete integer discriminant step $D_m = D_{\text{lo}} + 4m_{\text{step}} \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$ ($0 \leq m_{\text{step}} \leq dM$), certifying $(X, Y)$ as a guaranteed split prime (`is_guar = True`) across the integer discriminants $D_m \in [D_{\text{lo}}, D_{\text{hi}}]$ whenever $p_{\text{lo}}(X, Y) < M_{\text{lo}}^2$ or $(X, Y)$ is **absent** from `comp2_grid` at **every** $D_m$. Furthermore, at any fixed integer discriminant $D_* = D_{\text{lo}} + 4m_{\text{step}} \equiv 3 \pmod{4}$ in $[D_{\text{lo}}, D_{\text{hi}}]$, a true 2- or 3-prime composite norm $N_{D_*}(X, Y) = \prod_i N_{D_*}(x_i, y_i)$ in $\mathcal{O}_{K_{D_*}}$ has product coordinates $(X, Y)$ determined by complex multiplication at $D_*$. Because each individual factor norm $p_i(D) := N_D(x_i, y_i) = p_{i,\text{lo}} + \frac{D - D_{\text{lo}}}{4}y_i^2 \geq 0$ is non-decreasing in $D \in [D_{\text{lo}}, D_{\text{hi}}]$, evaluating at $D = D_*$ gives $p_{i,\text{lo}} \leq N_{D_*}(x_i, y_i) \leq p_{i,\text{hi}}$ for each factor $i$, and multiplying across the factors gives the **pointwise factorwise norm enclosure**
   $$
   m_{\text{lo}} := \prod_i p_{i,\text{lo}} \leq \prod_i N_{D_*}(x_i, y_i) = N_{D_*}(X, Y) \leq \prod_i p_{i,\text{hi}} =: m_{\text{hi}},
   $$
   together with $p_{\text{lo}}(X, Y) = N_{D_{\text{lo}}}(X, Y) \leq N_{D_*}(X, Y) \leq m_{\text{hi}}$ and $\frac{D_*}{4}Y^2 \leq N_{D_*}(X, Y) \leq m_{\text{hi}} \implies Y^2 \leq \lfloor 4 N_{D_*}(X, Y)/D_{\text{lo}}\rfloor \leq \lfloor 4 m_{\text{hi}}/D_{\text{lo}}\rfloor$, so the upper interval endpoint of the product pair $(X, Y)$ satisfies
   $$
   p_{\text{hi}}(X, Y) = N_{D_*}(X, Y) + \frac{D_{\text{hi}} - D_*}{4}Y^2 \leq m_{\text{hi}} + dM\left\lfloor \frac{4 m_{\text{hi}}}{D_{\text{lo}}}\right\rfloor \leq q_{\text{cap}},
   $$
   which guarantees slope-candidate inclusion $(X, Y) \in \mathcal{S}$ whenever $m_{\text{hi}} + dM\lfloor 4 m_{\text{hi}}/D_{\text{lo}}\rfloor \leq q_{\text{cap}}$. Decomposing $\mathcal{S}_{\text{prime}}(D_*) = \mathcal{S} \setminus \mathcal{S}_{\text{comp}}(D_*)$ directly: all $(x, y) \in \mathcal{S}$ are accumulated in a single pass, while the 2- and 3-prime slope composites ($m_{\text{lo}} \leq q_{\text{cap}}$) are subtracted from $\sigma_{2,\text{lo}}$ ($-\mu\frac{\log m_{\text{hi}}}{m_{\text{lo}}} \leq -\mu\frac{\log m_{\text{lo}}}{m_{\text{lo}}} \leq -\mu\frac{\log N_{D_*}(X,Y)}{N_{D_*}(X,Y)}$ since $N_{D_*}(X, Y) \geq m_{\text{lo}} > e$), $\sigma_{2,\text{hi}}$ ($-\mu\frac{\log m_{\text{lo}}}{m_{\text{hi}}} \geq -\mu\frac{\log N_{D_*}(X,Y)}{N_{D_*}(X,Y)}$ when `is_guar = True` and $m_{\text{hi}} + dM\lfloor 4 m_{\text{hi}}/D_{\text{lo}}\rfloor \leq q_{\text{cap}}$ so $(X, Y) \in \mathcal{S}$ is guaranteed), and `diff_blk_lo` ($-\mu\lfloor j_0/m_{\text{lo}}\rfloor\log m_{\text{hi}} \leq -\mu\lfloor j_0/N_{D_*}(X,Y)\rfloor\log N_{D_*}(X,Y)$ at $j_0 = 32 b_i$), and recursive norm-form composition certifies $k \in \{2, 3, 4\}$ UFD clusters with exact primitive representation multiplicity $2^{\#\text{distinct split primes}-1}$ (since $-p S'_r(R, K, p) - S_r(R, K, p) = p^2\big[kq(1-t) + \frac{q}{2}(q - 1 + 4t - 2t^2)\big] \geq 0$ for $R/p = q + t, \, t \in [0, 1), \, k = K/p$, $p \mapsto S_r(R, K, p)\log p$ is strictly decreasing on $[e, R]$, so $p_{\text{lo}}(X, Y) \leq N_{D_*}(X, Y) \leq m_{\text{hi}} \implies S_r(R, K, m_{\text{hi}})\log m_{\text{hi}} \leq S_r(R, K, N_{D_*}(X, Y))\log N_{D_*}(X, Y)$). Because $p_{\text{hi}}(x, y) = p_{\text{lo}}(x, y) + dM\,y^2$ can invert relative to $p_{\text{lo}}(x, y)$ when $dM > 0$, we sort `guaranteed_primes` in non-decreasing order of upper endpoints `guar_p_hi` and precompute the non-decreasing suffix minimum $\text{suf\_min\_lo}[r] := \min_{r \leq t < G}\text{guar\_p\_lo}[t]$ of the paired lower endpoints `guar_p_lo`: thus every upper-endpoint binary search (`bisect_right(guar_p_hi, ...)`) and every 16-element constant-quotient block $[r_0, r_1)$ in `sum_tail_blocks_flt` satisfies $m_a \leq m_r \leq m_b$ and $\lfloor R/m_r\rfloor = q$ identically across all $r \in [r_0, r_1)$ (evaluated over precomputed power-sum arrays `P1, P2, P3` via $\log m_r \geq \log m_a + \frac{m_r - m_a}{m_b}$ with directed int-to-float rounding `_NA(float(sum_sr), _NINF)` and `_NA(float(sum_sr_dm), _NINF)`), while every lower-endpoint cutoff and tail start index uses `suf_min_lo`.
