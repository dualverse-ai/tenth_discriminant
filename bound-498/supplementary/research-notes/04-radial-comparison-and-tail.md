### 5.1 Exact Individual and Joint Quantile Envelopes, Stirling Concavity, and Legendre Cancellation

**Lemma 5.1 (Exact Individual and Joint Quantile Envelopes on $\Lambda_{\text{tri}}$).** *Let $r_{(1)} \leq \cdots \leq r_{(M_{\text{tri}})}$, $s_{(1)} \leq \cdots \leq s_{(M_{\text{tri}})}$, and $(r+s)_{(1)} \leq \cdots \leq (r+s)_{(M_{\text{tri}})}$ denote the non-decreasing rearrangements of $r$, $s$, and $r+s$ on $\Lambda_{\text{tri}}$. Set $G_k := k / M_{\text{tri}} \in (0, 1]$ and $u_k := 1 - \sqrt{1 - G_k}$. Then for every $k \in \{1, \ldots, M_{\text{tri}}\}$:*

$$
R u_k - 1 \leq r_{(k)} \leq (R + K) u_k, \qquad S u_k - 1 \leq s_{(k)} \leq (S + 1) u_k, \qquad (r+s)_{(k)} \geq R u_{r+s}(G_k) - 2,
$$

*where $u_{r+s}(G) := \sqrt{G / K}$ for $0 \leq G \leq 1/K$ and $u_{r+s}(G) := 1 - \sqrt{\frac{K-1}{K}(1 - G)}$ for $1/K < G \leq 1$.*

*Proof.* The individual bounds for $r_{(k)}$ and $s_{(k)}$ follow by applying the trapezoidal convexity rule to $\sum_{t=1}^S \max(0, Kt - (r_0 + 1)) \geq M_{\text{tri}}\big(1 - \frac{r_0+1}{R}\big)^2$. For the joint sum $z = r + s$, on the scaled continuous triangle $T = \{x, y \geq 0 : x + Ky \leq 1\}$ of area $\frac{1}{2K}$, the sublevel set $\{x + y \leq u\}$ has area $\frac{u^2}{2}$ for $u \in [0, 1/K]$ (giving $G = K u^2$) and complement area $\frac{(1 - u)^2}{2(K - 1)}$ for $u \in [1/K, 1]$ (giving $1 - G = \frac{K}{K - 1}(1 - u)^2$). Accounting for the lattice boundary shift of at most $2$ yields $(r+s)_{(k)} \geq R u_{r+s}(G_k) - 2$. $\square$

In any non-zero Leibniz product $\prod_{k=1}^{M_{\text{tri}}} V_C^{\text{HS}}\big((r_k, s_k), (i_{\pi(k)}, j_{\pi(k)})\big)$, every factor has $\binom{r_k}{j_{\pi(k)}} \neq 0$, which forces $j_{\pi(k)} \leq r_k$. By the concavity of $\log(x)$, the falling factorial satisfies $\sum_{t=0}^{j-1} \log\big(\frac{r - t}{R}\big) \leq \int_0^j \log\big(\frac{r + 1 - t}{R}\big)\,dt = R\big[r_u \log r_u - (r_u - j_u)\log(r_u - j_u) - j_u\big]$, where $r_u := \min\big(1, \frac{r+1}{R}\big)$ and $j_u := \frac{j}{R} \in [0, r_u]$. On the $p$-adic side, because $K_{\text{drop}} = 0$, each column $(i, j) \in C$ pays $\sum_{p \leq J} \lfloor j/p\rfloor \log p$. Combining this with $-\log(j!)$ via Legendre's formula $j! = \prod_{p \leq j} p^{\sum_{\ell \geq 1} \lfloor j/p^\ell\rfloor}$ cancels the entire first-power prime sum $\sum_{p \leq j} \lfloor j/p\rfloor \log p$ **identically for all primes $p \geq 2$**, leaving only higher prime powers $\ell \geq 2$. For any prime-power cutoff $Q_0 \geq 1000$ (specifically $Q_0 := J - 1$ on every finite constant-parameter interval, which retains **all** non-zero prime-power floors $\lfloor j/p^\ell\rfloor$ since $\lfloor j/p^\ell\rfloor = 0$ for $p^\ell > J - 1 \geq j$, and $Q_0 := 1000$ on the unbounded infinite tail so zero new prime powers enter as $J \to \infty$), since $-\lfloor j/p^\ell\rfloor \log p \leq 0$ for $p^\ell > Q_0$:

$$
-\log(j!) + \sum_{p \leq J} \left\lfloor \frac{j}{p} \right\rfloor \log p \leq -\sum_{\substack{p \geq 2, \, \ell \geq 2 \\ p^\ell \leq Q_0}} \left\lfloor \frac{j}{p^\ell} \right\rfloor \log p = j\,\sigma_2(Q_0) - \sum_{\substack{p \geq 2, \, \ell \geq 2 \\ p^\ell \leq Q_0}} \left\lfloor \frac{j}{p^\ell} \right\rfloor \log p - j\,\sigma_2(Q_0), \qquad \sigma_2(Q_0) := \sum_{\substack{p \geq 2, \, \ell \geq 2 \\ p^\ell \leq Q_0}} \frac{\log p}{p^\ell}.
$$

Thus each column $(i, j) \in C$ has effective derivative slope $\text{slope}_j := \log(R/\rho_n) - \sigma_2(Q_0)$ and 1D remainder excess $H(j) := j\,\sigma_2(Q_0) - \sum_{p \geq 2, \ell \geq 2, p^\ell \leq Q_0}\lfloor j/p^\ell\rfloor\log p$ ($0 \leq j < J$). On the infinite tail ($Q_0 = 1000$), bounding each of the $25$ prime powers $q = p^\ell \leq 1000$ by filling its largest remainders $q-1, q-2, \ldots$ up to capacity $c_{\text{bin}}(q) := n\lceil J/q\rceil$ ($b_{\text{full}}(q) := \min(q, \lfloor M_{\text{tri}}/c_{\text{bin}}(q)\rfloor)$, $r_{\text{rem}}(q) := M_{\text{tri}} - b_{\text{full}}(q)c_{\text{bin}}(q)$) gives $\text{const}_j := 2\sum_{q = p^\ell \leq 1000} \frac{c_{\text{bin}}(q)(b_{\text{full}}(q)(q - 1) - \binom{b_{\text{full}}(q)}{2}) + r_{\text{rem}}(q)\max(0, q - 1 - b_{\text{full}}(q))}{M_{\text{tri}}\, q}\log p$, whereas on every finite interval ($Q_0 = J - 1$), sorting the 1D array $H(0), \ldots, H(J-1)$ in non-increasing order $H_{(1)} \geq \cdots \geq H_{(J)}$ (using the precomputed universal array $\text{PP\_FL}[j] := \sum_{p \geq 2, \ell \geq 2}\lfloor j/p^\ell\rfloor\log p$) bounds $\sum_{(i,j)\in C} 2 H(j) \leq M_{\text{tri}}\,\text{const}_j$ across all $|C| = M_{\text{tri}}$ via

$$
\text{const}_j := \frac{2}{M_{\text{tri}}}\left(n\sum_{m=1}^{\lfloor M_{\text{tri}}/n\rfloor} H_{(m)} + (M_{\text{tri}} \bmod n)\, H_{(\lfloor M_{\text{tri}}/n\rfloor + 1)}\right).
$$

To bound the linear derivative orders $j\,\text{slope}_j$, falling factorials $R f(r_u, j/R)$, and radial factors $(r+s-j)_+\log(\rho_n/\rho_i)$ **jointly** across all column selections $C \subset \{1, \ldots, n\} \times \{0, \ldots, J-1\}$ of size $M_{\text{tri}} = \theta n J$: let $c_i := \#\{j : (i, j) \in C\} \in \{0, \ldots, J\}$ and let $0 \leq j_{i,1} < \cdots < j_{i,c_i} \leq J-1$ be the chosen orders at $P_i$, so $j_{i,c_i+1-m} \leq J - m$ for $1 \leq m \leq c_i$. Let $j_{(1)} \leq \cdots \leq j_{(M_{\text{tri}})}$ be the non-decreasing rearrangement of $\{j : (i, j) \in C\}$ and let $j_{\text{top},(1)} \leq \cdots \leq j_{\text{top},(M_{\text{tri}})}$ be the non-decreasing rearrangement of the top-order multiset $\mathcal{J}_{\text{top}}(c) := \{J - m : 1 \leq i \leq n, 1 \leq m \leq c_i\}$. For every $k \in \{1, \ldots, M_{\text{tri}}\}$, at least $M_{\text{tri}} - k + 1$ elements of $\mathcal{J}_{\text{top}}(c)$ lie in $[j_{\text{top},(k)}, J-1]$ while each $P_i$ contributes at most $J - j_{\text{top},(k)}$ such elements, so $M_{\text{tri}} - k + 1 \leq n(J - j_{\text{top},(k)})$ and hence

$$
0 \leq j_{(k)} \leq j_{\text{top},(k)} \leq J - \frac{M_{\text{tri}} - k + 1}{n} < j_{\text{unif}}(u_k) := J - \frac{M_{\text{tri}} - k}{n} = \big(1 - \theta(1 - G_k)\big)J = \big(1 - \theta(1 - u_k)^2\big)J.
$$

Because $f(r_u, j_u) := r_u\log r_u - (r_u - j_u)\log(r_u - j_u) - j_u \leq 0$ has positive mixed partial $\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial r_u \partial j_u} = \frac{1}{r_u - j_u} > 0$ and is increasing in $r_u \leq r_u(u_k) := \min(1, (1 + \frac{1}{S})u_k + \frac{1}{R})$, Hardy–Littlewood–Lorentz rearrangement gives $\sum_{(i,j)\in C}\big[j\,\text{slope}_j + R f(\frac{r_{\pi^{-1}(i,j)}+1}{R}, \frac{j}{R})\big] \leq \sum_{k=1}^{M_{\text{tri}}} g_{\max}\big(r_u(u_k), j_{\text{top},(k)}\big)$, where $g_{\max}(r_u, x) := \max_{0 \leq \tilde{x} \leq \min(R r_u, x)}\big[\tilde{x}\,\text{slope}_j + R f(r_u, \tilde{x}/R)\big]$ is concave and non-decreasing in $x \geq 0$ with derivative $\frac{\partial g_{\max}}{\partial x}(r_u, x) = \big(\text{slope}_j + \log(r_u - x/R)\big)_+ \geq 0$. Evaluating at the uniform envelope $j_{\text{unif}}(u_k)$ on $M_{\text{steps}}$ equal $u$-bins $[u_m, u_{m+1}]$ ($u_m := m / M_{\text{steps}}$) defines the uniform falling-factorial saving

$$
\text{save}_{\text{deriv}} := 3\sum_{m=0}^{N_{\text{deriv}}-1} w_{\text{step},m}\big(j_{\text{low},m}\text{slope}_j - g_{\max,m}\big)_+,
$$

where on finite intervals ($N_{\text{deriv}} = 3,200$) we partition $\{1, \dots, M_{\text{tri}}\}$ into exact integer index blocks $(K_m, K_{m+1}]$ ($K_m := \lfloor M_{\text{tri}}(1 - (1 - m/N_{\text{deriv}})^2)\rfloor$, $w_{\text{step},m} := \frac{K_{m+1}-K_m}{M_{\text{tri}}}$, $G_{\text{lo},m} := \frac{K_m+1}{M_{\text{tri}}}$, $G_{\text{hi},m} := \frac{K_{m+1}}{M_{\text{tri}}}$, $u_{\text{hi},m} := 1 - \sqrt{1 - G_{\text{hi},m}}$, $j_{\text{low},m} := \max(0, 1 - \theta(1 - G_{\text{lo},m}))\frac{J}{R}$, $r_{u,m} := \min(1, (1 + \frac{1}{S})u_{\text{hi},m} + \frac{1}{R})$) with zero discretization loss, while on the infinite tail ($N_{\text{deriv}} = 3,200$, $u_m := m / N_{\text{deriv}}$) we use the exact difference of squares $w_{\text{step},m} := \max\big(0, (1 - u_m)^2 - (1 - u_{m+1})^2 - \frac{1}{M_{\text{tri}}}\big)$ and $j_{\text{low},m} := \max(0, (1 - \theta_{\text{ub}}(1 - u_m)^2)\varepsilon_J^{\text{lo}})$ (and on hybrid intervals $M_{\text{steps}} = 800$), with $j^*_m := \max\big(0, \min(j_{\text{low},m}, r_{u,m} - e^{-\text{slope}_j})\big)$ and $g_{\max,m} := \max(0, j^*_m\text{slope}_j + f(r_{u,m}, j^*_m))$ for $j^*_m > 0$ ($0$ otherwise).

### 5.2 Staircase Concavity-Ramp Coupling, Exact Pascal Hockey-Stick Identity, and Convex Variational Duality

**Lemma 5.2 (Staircase Concavity-Ramp Variational Duality and Pascal Hockey-Stick Identity).** *Choose $n$ as an exact integer multiple of $M_{\text{steps}}$ ($m_{\text{bin}} := n / M_{\text{steps}} \in \mathbb{Z}$) and let $C \subset \{1, \ldots, n\} \times \{0, \ldots, J-1\}$ have cardinality $|C| = M_{\text{tri}} = \theta n J$ with $\theta\frac{J}{R} < \frac{1}{2}$. For $L_{\text{steps}} := 120$, set $m_{\text{start}} := \max(1, \lfloor 0.02 J\rfloor)$ and $m_{\text{end}} := \max(m_{\text{start}} + 1, \min(\lfloor M_{\text{tri}}/n\rfloor - 1, \lfloor 0.495 J\rfloor))$ on finite intervals (or $m_{\text{start}}^0 := \max(1, \lfloor 0.02 J_{\text{lo}}\rfloor)$ and $m_{\text{end}}^0 := \lfloor 0.46 J_{\text{lo}}\rfloor$ on the infinite tail), define the candidate integer cuts $m_r := m_{\text{start}} + \lfloor \frac{(m_{\text{end}} - m_{\text{start}})(r-1)}{L_{\text{steps}}-1}\rfloor$ ($1 \leq r \leq L_{\text{steps}}$, skipping any duplicate $m_r$, or $m_r(D) := \lceil \tau_{\text{base},r} J(D)\rceil$ with $\tau_{\text{base},r} := m_r^0 / J_{\text{lo}}$ on the infinite tail), and stop at the last index $L \leq L_{\text{steps}}$ where the admissibility conditions $G_r := 1 - \frac{n m_r}{M_{\text{tri}}} > 0$, $\text{arg}_r := 1 - \sqrt{1 - G_r} - (1 - \tau_r)\frac{J}{R} > 0$ ($\tau_r := m_r / J$), and $\nu_r := \text{slope}_j + \log(\text{arg}_r) > 0$ all hold strictly. Setting $\nu_{L+1} := 0$ and retaining all steps $1 \leq r \leq L$ with positive increment $\Delta\nu_r := (\nu_r - \nu_{r+1})_+ > 0$, one has:*

1. *Because $\theta\frac{J}{R} < \frac{1}{2}$, the function $h(G) := 1 - \sqrt{1 - G} - (1 - \theta(1 - G))\frac{J}{R}$ is strictly increasing on $[0, 1]$ ($h'(G) = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{1-G}} - \theta\frac{J}{R} \geq \frac{1}{2} - \theta\frac{J}{R} > 0$). Thus for every $k \in \{1, \ldots, M_{\text{tri}}\}$, concavity of $x \mapsto g_{\max}(r_u(u_k), x)$ on $[j_{\text{top},(k)}, j_{\text{unif}}(u_k)]$ implies $\frac{\partial g_{\max}}{\partial x}(r_u(u_k), x) \geq \sum_{r=1}^L \Delta\nu_r \mathbf{1}_{x \geq J - m_r}$ on $[j_{\text{top},(k)}, j_{\text{unif}}(u_k)]$, and hence*
   $$
   g_{\max}\big(r_u(u_k), j_{\text{top},(k)}\big) \leq g_{\max}\big(r_u(u_k), j_{\text{unif}}(u_k)\big) - \sum_{r=1}^L \Delta\nu_r\Big[\big(j_{\text{unif}}(u_k) - (J - m_r)\big)_+ - \big(j_{\text{top},(k)} - (J - m_r)\big)_+\Big].
   $$
   *Summing over $k = 1, \ldots, M_{\text{tri}}$ using $\sum_{k=1}^{M_{\text{tri}}} j_{\text{unif}}(u_k) = M_{\text{tri}} J(1 - \frac{1}{2}\theta) + \frac{1}{2}\theta J$ and the exact multiset identity $\sum_{k=1}^{M_{\text{tri}}}\big[\big(j_{\text{unif}}(u_k) - (J - m_r)\big)_+ - \big(j_{\text{top},(k)} - (J - m_r)\big)_+\big] = \frac{(n+1)m_r}{2} + \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^n (m_r - c_i)_+(m_r - c_i - 1) \geq \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^n (m_r - c_i)_+^2$, while on the radial term bounding $\log(\rho_n/\rho_i)$ below by the clipped radial weight $\tilde{\ell}_i := \min\big(\log(\rho_n/\rho_i), L_{b_0}\big) \geq L_{\max(b(i), b_0)}$ ($b_0 := \lfloor 0.60 M_{\text{steps}}\rfloor$) and applying $(r_k + s_k - j)_+ \geq (r_k + s_k - J)_+ + (J - j) - (J - r_k - s_k)_+$ (with $J - j_{i,c_i+1-m} \geq m$) and the **exact integer Pascal hockey-stick identity***
   $$
   \sum_{(r,s)\in\Lambda_{\text{tri}}} (J - r - s)_+ \leq \sum_{s=0}^{S-1}\sum_{r \geq 0} (J - r - s)_+ = \sum_{s=0}^{S-1} \binom{(J - s)_+ + 1}{2} = \binom{J + 2}{3} - \binom{(J - S)_+ + 2}{3}
   $$
   *(with exact equality whenever $J \leq K + S - 1$) recover $+\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^n c_i^2 \tilde{\ell}_i - L_{b_0}\Delta_0\frac{W_{\text{main}}}{2}$ with **zero Riemann-sum discretization error**, where for all $J \geq S$:*
   $$
   \Delta_0(R, J) := \frac{2\big(\binom{J+2}{3} - \binom{(J-S)_+ + 2}{3}\big)}{W_{\text{main}}} = \frac{J(J+1)(J+2) - (J-S)(J-S+1)(J-S+2)}{R^2 S} = 3\left(\frac{J + 1}{R}\right)^2 - \frac{3}{K}\left(\frac{J + 1}{R}\right) + \frac{1}{K^2} - \frac{1}{R^2}.
   $$
   *(In particular, since $u \mapsto 3u^2 - \frac{3}{K}u + \frac{1}{K^2}$ is strictly increasing for $u > \frac{1}{2K}$, on the infinite tail $D \geq D_{\text{tail}}$ where $R(D) \geq R_{\text{lo}}$, $J_{\text{lo}} = \lfloor \varepsilon_J R_{\text{lo}}\rfloor$, and $J_{\text{hi,eff}} := J_{\text{lo}} + 1 > \varepsilon_J R_{\text{lo}}$, one has $\frac{J(D)+1}{R(D)} \leq \varepsilon_J + \frac{1}{R_{\text{lo}}} < \frac{J_{\text{hi,eff}}+1}{R_{\text{lo}}}$ and $\Delta_0(R(D), J(D)) \leq 3(\varepsilon_J + \frac{1}{R_{\text{lo}}})^2 - \frac{3}{K}(\varepsilon_J + \frac{1}{R_{\text{lo}}}) + \frac{1}{K^2} < \Delta_0(R_{\text{lo}}, J_{\text{hi,eff}}) =: \Delta_0^{\text{tail}}$.)*
2. *Partition $\{1, \ldots, n\}$ into $M_{\text{steps}}$ equal integer blocks $I_b := \{b m_{\text{bin}} + 1, \ldots, (b+1)m_{\text{bin}}\}$ ($0 \leq b < M_{\text{steps}}$) of exact cardinality $|I_b| = m_{\text{bin}}$, with bin density $w_b := \frac{1}{m_{\text{bin}} J}\sum_{i \in I_b} c_i \in [0, 1]$, cumulative quantile $G_b(w) := \frac{1}{\theta M_{\text{steps}}}\sum_{a=0}^b w_a \in [0, 1]$, and right-endpoint radial bound $L_b(D) := \log\big(\frac{\rho_n(D)}{\sqrt{\min(1, \frac{b+1}{M_{\text{steps}}} + \frac{1}{n})}\text{base\_rad}(D) + \delta_D}\big)$ ($L_{M_{\text{steps}}} := 0$). Because $\eta_D = \frac{\delta_D}{\text{base\_rad}(D)}$ is strictly increasing in $D$ for $D > 4$ with fixed $n$, $L_b(D)$ is strictly decreasing in $D$, so on any interval $[D_{\text{lo}}, D_{\text{hi}}]$ the positive radial terms $L_b - L_{b+1}$ and $L_{\max(b,b_0)}w_b^2$ are bounded below at $D_{\text{hi}}$ ($L_b^{\text{lo}} := L_b(D_{\text{hi}})$) while the negative term $-L_{b_0}\Delta_0$ is bounded below at $D_{\text{lo}}$ ($L_{b_0}^{\text{hi}} := L_{b_0}(D_{\text{lo}})$, or $L_{b_0}^{\text{hi}} := \frac{1}{2}\log\big(\frac{M_{\text{steps}}}{b_0+1}\big)$ at $\eta = 0$ on the infinite tail). Define the smooth convex joint radial function $\Phi_{\text{joint}}(G) := 3\int_0^G \big(u_{r+s}(Q) - u_{0,\text{joint}}\big)_+\,dQ$ ($u_{0,\text{joint}} := \frac{J+2}{R}$, closed-form antiderivative via $\int (1 - \sqrt{\alpha(1-Q)} - u_0)\,dQ = \frac{2}{\alpha}\big[\frac{1+u_0}{2}t^2 - \frac{1}{3}t^3 - u_0 t\big]_{t=1-\sqrt{\alpha(1-Q)}}$, and derivative $B_{\text{joint}}(G) := \Phi'_{\text{joint}}(G) = 3(u_{r+s}(G) - u_{0,\text{joint}})_+$). Then for every admissible column set $C$, with **zero bin-discretization error**:*
   $$
   \frac{2}{W_{\text{main}}}\sum_{(i,j)\in C}\left[j\,\text{slope}_j + R f\!\left(\frac{r_{\pi^{-1}(i,j)}+1}{R}, \frac{j}{R}\right)\right] - \frac{2}{W_{\text{main}}}\sum_{k=1}^{M_{\text{tri}}} \big(r_k + s_k - j_{\pi(k)}\big)_+ \log\!\left(\frac{\rho_n}{\rho_{i_{\pi(k)}}}\right) \leq \gamma_{\text{unif}}\text{slope}_j - \mathcal{F}(w) - \text{save}_{\text{deriv}},
   $$
   *where $\gamma_{\text{unif}} := \frac{2}{W_{\text{main}}}\big(M_{\text{tri}} J(1 - \frac{1}{2}\theta) + \frac{1}{2}\theta J\big)$ and $\mathcal{F}(w) := \sum_{b=0}^{M_{\text{steps}}-1} (L_b^{\text{lo}} - L_{b+1}^{\text{lo}})\Phi_{\text{joint}}\big(G_b(w)\big) + \frac{1}{M_{\text{steps}}}\sum_{b=0}^{M_{\text{steps}}-1} \frac{3(J/R)}{2\theta}\Big[L_{\max(b,b_0)}^{\text{lo}} w_b^2 + \sum_{r=1}^L \Delta\nu_r(\tau_r - w_b)_+^2\Big] - L_{b_0}^{\text{hi}}\Delta_0$ is a **convex functional** on the simplex $\mathcal{P} := \{w \in [0, 1]^{M_{\text{steps}}} : \sum_{b=0}^{M_{\text{steps}}-1} w_b = \theta M_{\text{steps}}\}$.*
3. *Because $\frac{\partial G_b(w)}{\partial w_a} = \frac{1}{\theta M_{\text{steps}}}\mathbf{1}_{a \leq b}$ and $\frac{1}{2}(\tau_r - v_a)_+^2 \geq \frac{1}{2}(\tau_r - w^*_a)_+^2 - (\tau_r - w^*_a)_+(v_a - w^*_a)$, at any rational witness $w^*_a = k_a / D_{\text{den}} \in \mathcal{P}$ ($\sum_a k_a = N_{\text{num}} = \theta M_{\text{steps}} D_{\text{den}}$) the exact supporting-hyperplane subgradient is*
   $$
   (\nabla\mathcal{F}(w^*))_a = \frac{1}{M_{\text{steps}}}\left[\sum_{b=a}^{M_{\text{steps}}-1} \frac{(L_b^{\text{lo}} - L_{b+1}^{\text{lo}}) B_{\text{joint}}\big(G_b(w^*)\big)}{\theta} + \frac{3(J/R)}{\theta}\Big(L_{\max(a,b_0)}^{\text{lo}} w^*_a - \sum_{r=1}^L \Delta\nu_r(\tau_r - w^*_a)_+\Big)\right],
   $$
   *and hence for **every** admissible column set $C$ (whose induced bin-density vector $v = w(C)$ lies in $\mathcal{P}$), $\mathcal{F}(w(C)) \geq \min_{v \in \mathcal{P}} \mathcal{F}(v) \geq \mathcal{F}_{\text{lb}}(w^*) := \mathcal{F}(w^*) - \max_{v \in \mathcal{P}} \langle \nabla\mathcal{F}(w^*), w^* - v\rangle$, where $\max_{v \in \mathcal{P}} \langle \nabla\mathcal{F}(w^*), w^* - v\rangle$ is solved in closed form by greedily filling $v_{\min,a} = m_a / D_{\text{den}} \in [0, 1]$ ($\sum_a m_a = N_{\text{num}}$) in non-decreasing order of $(\nabla\mathcal{F}(w^*))_a$.*

*Proof.* **Part (1) (Staircase concavity-ramp coupling and Pascal hockey-stick identity):** For each step $r \in \{1, \ldots, L\}$, $j_{\text{unif}}(u_k) = (1 - \theta(1 - G_k))J \geq J - m_r \iff G_k \geq 1 - \frac{n m_r}{M_{\text{tri}}} = G_r$, so monotonicity of $h(G)$ on $[G_r, 1]$ gives $r_u(u_k) - j_{\text{unif}}(u_k)/R \geq h(G_k) \geq h(G_r) = \text{arg}_r > 0$ and $\frac{\partial g_{\max}}{\partial x}(r_u(u_k), x) \geq \nu_r$ for $x \in [J - m_r, j_{\text{unif}}(u_k)]$. Writing $s = M_{\text{tri}} - k \in \{0, \ldots, n m_r - 1\}$ gives $\sum_{k=1}^{M_{\text{tri}}}(j_{\text{unif}}(u_k) - (J - m_r))_+ = \sum_{s=0}^{n m_r - 1}(m_r - s/n) = \frac{n m_r^2 + m_r}{2}$, while on $\mathcal{J}_{\text{top}}(c) = \{J - m : 1 \leq i \leq n, 1 \leq m \leq c_i\}$ we have $\sum_{k=1}^{M_{\text{tri}}}(j_{\text{top},(k)} - (J - m_r))_+ = \sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{m=1}^{c_i}(m_r - m)_+ = \frac{n m_r(m_r - 1)}{2} - \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^n (m_r - c_i)_+(m_r - c_i - 1)$. Subtracting the two identities and using $\sum_{i=1}^n (m_r - c_i)_+ \leq n m_r$ gives $\frac{(n+1)m_r}{2} + \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^n ((m_r - c_i)_+^2 - (m_r - c_i)_+) \geq \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^n (m_r - c_i)_+^2$, and summing with weights $\Delta\nu_r > 0$ yields the staircase ramp. On the radial term, bounding $\log(\rho_n/\rho_{i_{\pi(k)}}) \geq \tilde{\ell}_{i_{\pi(k)}} := \min(\log(\rho_n/\rho_{i_{\pi(k)}}), L_{b_0})$ and decomposing $(r_k + s_k - j)_+ \geq (r_k + s_k - J)_+ + (J - j) - (J - r_k - s_k)_+$ gives $\sum_{m=1}^{c_i} (J - j_{i,c_i+1-m})\tilde{\ell}_i \geq \frac{1}{2}c_i^2 \tilde{\ell}_i \geq \frac{1}{2}c_i^2 L_{\max(b(i), b_0)}^{\text{lo}}$, while since $\tilde{\ell}_i \leq L_{b_0} \leq L_{b_0}^{\text{hi}}$ and $\Lambda_{\text{tri}} \subset \{r \geq 0, \, 0 \leq s \leq S - 1\}$ (with every $(r, s)$ with $r + s \leq J$ lying inside $\Lambda_{\text{tri}}$ whenever $J \leq K + S - 1$), the Pascal hockey-stick identity $\sum_{m=0}^N \binom{m+1}{2} = \binom{N+2}{3}$ gives $\sum_{(r,s)\in\Lambda_{\text{tri}}}(J - r - s)_+ \leq \binom{J+2}{3} - \binom{(J-S)_+ + 2}{3}$, and expanding $(J+1)^3 - (J+1) - ((J-S+1)^3 - (J-S+1)) = 3S(J+1)^2 - 3S^2(J+1) + S^3 - S$ for $J \geq S$ yields the exact polynomial identity for $\Delta_0(R, J)$. Parts (2) and (3) follow identically by Cauchy–Schwarz on each block $I_b$ ($\sum_{i \in I_b}(m_r - c_i)_+^2 \geq m_{\text{bin}} J^2(\tau_r - w_b)_+^2$), Abel summation over $b$, and supporting-hyperplane convexity of $\Phi_{\text{joint}}(G_b(v))$, $v_b^2$, and $(\tau_r - v_b)_+^2$ on $\mathcal{P}$. $\square$

**Prime-Sum Derivations from Rosser–Schoenfeld [10]:** For every $p \leq P_{\max}$, $p^2\log p \leq P_{\max} p\log p$, so $\sum_{K \leq p \leq P_{\max}}\left(\frac{p}{2R^2} - \frac{p^2}{12\sqrt{3}R^3}\right)\log p \geq \left(\frac{1}{2R^2} - \frac{P_{\max}}{12\sqrt{3}R^3}\right)\sum_{K \leq p \leq P_{\max}} p\log p$.
